How to Study

Test your knowledge of the 5 Seeing Habits. Read each question, think of your answer, then flip the card to check yourself.

  1. Read the question.
  2. Think of your answer before flipping the card.
  3. Flip the card to reveal the answer.
  4. Mark your answer correct or incorrect.
  5. Continue until you complete all five habits.
